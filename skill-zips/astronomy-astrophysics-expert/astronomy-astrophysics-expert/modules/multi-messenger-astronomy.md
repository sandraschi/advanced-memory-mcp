# Multi-Messenger Astronomy

**Confidence**: 🟢 HIGH
**Last updated**: 2025-11-09
**Primary references**: [LIGO-News-2025], [Webb-ESA-2025]

## Current state
- **Gravitational waves**: LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA O4 run (2023–2025) has surpassed 200 detections, including the most massive black hole merger (>180 M☉ total) and improved localisation for neutron-star mergers (<50 deg² in select events).
- **Neutrinos**: IceCube upgrades increase sensitivity to TeV–PeV sources; joint alerts with LIGO & EM facilities support real-time follow-up.
- **Electromagnetic counterparts**: JWST and ground-based observatories (Gemini, VLT, LSST pre-ops) capture kilonovae across NIR/optical, constraining r-process yields.

## Coordination workflow
1. **Trigger ingestion** – parse GraceDB notices (GW), GCN Circulars (gamma-ray, neutrino).
2. **Localisation** – map probability skymaps, prioritise for telescope scheduling, consider galaxy-targeted strategies.
3. **Follow-up** – recommend appropriate instruments (e.g., JWST NIRCam for NIR, LSST for optical).
4. **Data synthesis** – combine GW strain data, spectral evolution, and high-energy observations to characterise progenitors.

## Quick reference
- Typical GW detection latency: 30–90 seconds for preliminary alert.
- Neutron-star merger ejecta masses ~0.04–0.06 M☉ with velocities 0.1–0.3c.
- Multi-messenger successes: GW170817 (baseline), GW190521 (massive BH merger), O4 detections pushing mass limits.
