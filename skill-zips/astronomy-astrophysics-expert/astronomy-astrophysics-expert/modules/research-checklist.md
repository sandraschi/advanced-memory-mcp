# Research Checklist & Source Log

Follow these steps to keep the astronomy skill current:

1. Identify freshness risks (new mission results, catalogue releases, parameter updates).  
2. Run targeted searches across ESA, NASA, ESO, LIGO, and peer-reviewed sources dated 2024 onwards.  
3. Record every authoritative source in the log below with access date and short annotation.  
4. Update the relevant modules and adjust confidence statements when new evidence arrives.  
5. Revisit [modules/known-gaps.md](modules/known-gaps.md) to close resolved items or add new uncertainties.  
6. Regenerate skill archives (`scripts/maintenance/rebuild_skill_archives.py`) after substantial edits.  
7. Document pipeline or archive versioning (e.g., JWST CRDS context, Euclid processing release).
8. Where deeper synthesis is required, leverage in-house helpers: `adn_skills("distill_from_wikipedia", ...)`, `adn_skills("distill_from_arxiv", ...)`, and related distillation tools to bootstrap literature reviews.
