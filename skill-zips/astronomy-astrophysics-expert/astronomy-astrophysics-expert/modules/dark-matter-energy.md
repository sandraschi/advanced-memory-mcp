# Dark Matter & Dark Energy

**Confidence**: 🟢 HIGH
**Last updated**: 2025-11-09
**Primary references**: [Euclid-ESA-2025], [ESO-Quasar-2024]

## Observational pillars
- **Weak lensing & cosmic shear**: Euclid deep fields and wide survey quantify dark matter distribution with high-resolution shape measurements over 15,000 deg².
- **Galaxy rotation curves & clusters**: Classic evidence via flat rotation curves, Bullet Cluster lensing; current surveys refine mass models.
- **Large-scale structure**: BAO peak positions track expansion history; Euclid + DESI + Planck synergy reduces parameter degeneracies.

## Dark energy diagnostics
| Probe | Key metric |
| --- | --- |
| Supernovae Ia | Distance modulus vs redshift, equation-of-state parameter w. |
| BAO | Angular diameter distances and H(z). |
| Growth rate | fσ₈ derived from redshift-space distortions. |
| Quasar & AGN | High-z luminosity functions (e.g., ESO J0529-4351) stress-test early SMBH growth and energy injection. |

## Assistant guidance
1. State the **evidence chain** before diving into theoretical candidates.
2. Differentiate between **dark matter** (missing mass) and **dark energy** (accelerated expansion).
3. Summarise leading particle candidates (WIMPs, axions) vs. alternatives (MOND, modified gravity) and their observational status.
4. Highlight **upcoming experiments** (Euclid operations, Rubin LSST, CMB-S4, LISA) that will improve constraints.
5. Provide **numerical constraints**: e.g., w = –1.0 ± 0.03 (current combined fits), Ω_DM ≈ 0.27.

## Quick facts
- Euclid aims for σ(w) < 0.02 when combined with Planck.
- Cluster mass calibration relies on weak lensing + X-ray/SZ measurements; note systematics (baryonic feedback).
- Direct detection experiments (XENONnT, LZ) currently probe spin-independent cross-sections down to ~6×10^–48 cm² for 40 GeV WIMPs.
