**Confidence**: 🟢 HIGH

## Current landscape
- **Inner planets**: Artemis-supporting observations focus on volatile distribution (e.g., Lunar Trailblazer, CLPS payloads) and updated thermal-infrared mapping of Mercury (BepiColombo flybys).
- **Mars**: Perseverance sample caching status (21 cores stored as of Oct 2025), Ingenuity retired with 72 flights, ESA/ROSCosmos ExoMars still awaiting launch window; note upcoming MSR architecture revisions.
- **Gas/ice giants**: Juno’s extended mission mapping of Jupiter’s polar cyclones, JUICE en route (arrival 2031) with gravity assists underway; mention recent JWST atmospheric spectroscopy of Saturn’s rings and Uranus storms.
- **Small bodies**: OSIRIS-REx sample return analysis (carbonaceous material with hydrated minerals), DART follow-up via Hera (arrives 2026) to characterise kinetic impact outcomes.

## Module usage tips
- Provide **comparative planetology** context: link atmospheric dynamics, interior structure, and magnetic fields to habitability or space weather implications.
- Highlight **mission status**: use up-to-date milestones (e.g., JUICE Earth flyby Aug 2024, Europa Clipper launch Oct 2024).
- Emphasise **data availability**: direct users to NASA PDS, ESA PSA, and planetary data portals for terrain models or spectral cubes.

## Checklists
1. State the **mission context** and observation date.
2. Compare with **previous baselines** (e.g., Cassini vs JWST Saturn data).
3. Provide uncertainties or competing hypotheses.
4. Suggest **next missions** or instruments for follow-up.
5. Note cross-discipline synergies (e.g., Solar Orbiter data informing comet tail modelling).
