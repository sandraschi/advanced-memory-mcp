### 3. 🚀 Revolutionary Knowledge Platform

**The Problem**: Knowledge tools are dumb, one-size-fits-all, low quality

**The Solution**: Intelligent platform with AI curation, quality control, and personalization

**Documentation**: 1 guide, 1,380 lines
1. ZETTELKASTEN_PLATFORM_VISION.md (1,380 lines)

**The Six Pillars**:

#### Pillar 1: Comprehensive User Questionnaire
- **Profession & specialization**: What you do
- **Interests & hobbies**: What you love
- **Experience levels**: Beginner to expert
- **Learning style**: How you learn best
- **Controversy preferences**: How to handle sensitive topics
- **LLM assistance**: Which AI to use

**5-10 minute personalized onboarding**

#### Pillar 2: Multi-LLM Integration
- **Claude**: Premium quality ($$$)
- **FOSS LLMs**: Llama3, Mistral, Qwen, DeepSeek (free)
- **Hybrid**: Claude for critical + FOSS for bulk

**Use cases**:
- Generate summaries of topics
- Create practice questions
- Suggest related topics
- Update content automatically
- Translate content
- Create visual diagrams

#### Pillar 3: 4-Layer Quality Control
1. **Content Validation**: Length, structure, links, citations, readability
2. **Source Verification**: Tiered credibility (Tier 1: academic, Tier 2: reputable, Blacklist: unreliable)
3. **Anti-Slop Detection**: Marketing speak, AI tells, vague language, filler
4. **Community Moderation**: Peer review, reputation system, expert verification

**Result**: Zero slop, high-quality content only

#### Pillar 4: Meta-Zettelkasten
**"The Zettelkasten of Zettelkastens"**

Catalog of knowledge domains with:
- **Educational level parameters**:
  - K-12: elementary, middle_school, high_school
  - Higher Ed: undergraduate, graduate, doctoral
  - Professional: beginner, intermediate, advanced, expert
  - Specialized: hobbyist, professional, researcher, educator

- **Templates for every subject**:
  - Programming (Python beginner → expert)
  - Science (high school → doctoral research)
  - Arts (hobbyist → professional artist)
  - Business (entrepreneur → MBA)

**Appropriate depth for every level**

#### Pillar 5: Curated Web Resources (200+ sites)
- **Entertainment**: TVTropes (storytelling patterns), IMDb
- **Technology**: Stack Overflow, GitHub, Hacker News
- **Learning**: Khan Academy, Coursera, Wikipedia, Archive.org
- **Productivity**: Notion, Obsidian forums
- **Creative**: Behance (design), Unsplash (photos)

**Integration**: Create notes for each resource with usage guides

#### Pillar 6: Controversy Handling
**Balanced approach framework**:

- **Classification**: Safe, Mild, Moderate, High, Sensitive
- **User preferences**:
  - Include all perspectives (balanced coverage)
  - Mainstream views only
  - Exclude controversial entirely
  - Let me choose per-topic

- **Balanced coverage**:
  - Multi-perspective notes
  - Common ground identification
  - Key disagreements analysis
  - Critical thinking questions

**No propaganda, just balanced information**
