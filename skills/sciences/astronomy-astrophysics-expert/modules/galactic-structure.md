# Galactic Structure & Dynamics

**Confidence**: 🟢 HIGH  
**Last updated**: 2025-11-09  
**Primary references**: [Euclid-ESA-2025], [ESO-Quasar-2024]

## Milky Way insights
- Gaia DR3 + spectroscopy map stellar streams (Sagittarius, GD-1) revealing dark matter substructure.  
- APOGEE + GALAH chemical tagging reconstructs star-formation history and radial migration patterns.  
- JWST resolves star-forming clumps in the Galactic Centre, improving understanding of bar-driven inflows and SMBH feeding.

## Extragalactic advances
- **Euclid**: March 2025 first data release includes deep fields with >26 million galaxies, enabling cosmic shear measurements for dark matter mapping and baryon acoustic oscillation (BAO) distance scales out to z~2.  
- **ESO/VLT**: SPHERE & MUSE studies of high-z galaxies show morphological diversity and kinematic asymmetries; luminous quasars like J0529-4351 provide constraints on early SMBH seed growth.  
- **JWST**: NIRCam/NIRSpec reveals galaxies at z>10 with complex morphologies, challenging rapid enrichment assumptions.

## Quick numbers
- Milky Way stellar mass ~5–6 ×10^10 M☉; dark matter halo ~1–1.5 ×10^12 M☉.  
- Euclid wide survey: 15,000 deg² at 0.3″ resolution; deep fields: 40 deg², deeper by ~2 mag.  
- Typical high-redshift galaxy rotation speeds 100–300 km/s, often with turbulent dispersion ~50–80 km/s indicating clumpy accretion phases.
