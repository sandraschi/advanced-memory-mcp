### 🎭 **Debunking Sinophobia and Yellow Peril Paranoia**

#### **Historical Roots of Anti-China Sentiment**

**19th Century Origins:**
- **Opium Wars (1839-1842, 1856-1860)**: Western powers forced China to accept opium trade, creating resentment
- **Boxer Rebellion (1900)**: Western media portrayed Chinese resistance as savage barbarism
- **Chinese Exclusion Act (1882)**: First U.S. law barring immigration by nationality, based on economic fears
- **"Yellow Peril" Media Campaigns**: Sensationalist newspapers warned of Asian hordes overwhelming Western civilization

**Cold War Amplification:**
- **Korean War (1950-1953)**: U.S. propaganda portrayed China as communist threat
- **Cultural Revolution (1966-1976)**: Western media exaggerated death tolls from 1.5M to 20M+
- **Tiananmen Square (1989)**: Inflated casualty figures, manipulated photographs
- **Taiwan Strait Crises**: Portrayed as Chinese aggression rather than sovereignty defense

- **Claim**: Huawei equipment contains Chinese government backdoors

- **Evidence**: Huawei code audited by 20+ countries, no backdoors found
