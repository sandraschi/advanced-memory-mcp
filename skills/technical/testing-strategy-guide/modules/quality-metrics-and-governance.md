# Quality Metrics & Governance

**Confidence**: 🟡 Medium  
**Last validated**: 2025-11-08  
**Primary sources**: Accelerate 2024, SPACE Framework (2025 update), Atlassian Quality Dashboard Playbook (2024)

---

## 1. Key Metrics

- Lead time for changes, deployment frequency, change failure rate, MTTR.  
- Test coverage (unit/integration/e2e), mutation score, flake rate.  
- Escaped defects, customer support tickets, NPS/CSAT.  
- Track per team/service; review in engineering forums.

---

## 2. Quality Gates

- Define gating criteria (coverage thresholds, lint checks, vulnerability scans).  
- Enforce via CI pipelines and merge requirements.  
- Allow controlled overrides with documented sign-off.

---

## 3. Dashboards & Reporting

- Use analytics tools (Looker, Tableau, Datadog) to visualize trends.  
- Present weekly snapshots to leadership; highlight wins and risks.  
- Maintain scorecards for squads (green/yellow/red status).

---

## 4. Process Governance

- Establish quality council or community of practice.  
- Conduct regular retros focused on testing effectiveness.  
- Update handbook with testing standards and review annually.

---

## 5. Continuous Improvement

- Run experiments (e.g., contract tests introduction) and measure impact.  
- Celebrate success stories to encourage adoption.  
- Maintain roadmap for testing platform enhancements.

---

### Checklist
- [ ] Metrics defined, automated, and visible.  
- [ ] Quality gates enforced with override process.  
- [ ] Dashboards/reporting aligned with stakeholders.  
- [ ] Governance forum active with regular reviews.  
- [ ] Continuous improvement backlog maintained.

Governance keeps testing strategy aligned with business goals and engineering productivity.***
