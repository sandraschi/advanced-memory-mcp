# Change Management & Communication

**Confidence**: 🟡 Medium  
**Last validated**: 2025-11-08  
**Primary sources**: Accelerate 2024 (Change Enablement), Atlassian Team Playbook (2025), GitHub Engineering Blog Change Management (2024)

---

## 1. Stakeholder Mapping

- Identify sponsors (engineering leadership, product, platform).  
- Map affected teams; assign refactoring ambassadors.  
- Clarify decision-making and escalation paths.

---

## 2. Communication Plan

- Kick-off presentation outlining why, scope, benefits.  
- Weekly async updates (Slack/Confluence) with metrics and risks.  
- Office hours or Q&A sessions for impacted teams.  
- Post-refactoring summary with before/after metrics.

---

## 3. Rollout Strategy

- Slice work into incremental deliverables; avoid long-lived branches.  
- Use feature flags toggling new structure until ready.  
- Plan freezes only when necessary; communicate timelines clearly.  
- Provide migration guides for downstream consumers.

---

## 4. Change Enablement

- Provide training, coding guidelines, and sample patterns.  
- Pair/mob sessions to spread knowledge.  
- Update documentation, code owners, and onboarding materials.  
- Celebrate wins to maintain momentum.

---

## 5. Risk Mitigation

- Maintain risk register (technical, organizational).  
- Define rollback criteria; monitor leading indicators (burn-down, bug count).  
- Schedule retros for each milestone to adjust approach.

---

### Checklist
- [ ] Stakeholder map and communication plan documented.  
- [ ] Incremental rollout plan with flags/backwards compatibility.  
- [ ] Training and documentation updates scheduled.  
- [ ] Risk register maintained with mitigation actions.  
- [ ] Retrospectives conducted and findings acted upon.

Refactoring success depends as much on people and communication as on technical skill.***
