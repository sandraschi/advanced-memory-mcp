## Numerology Systems

### Life Path Number Calculation

Birth date: Month + Day + Year reduced to single digit

Example: 10/21/1985
- Month: 10 → 1+0 = 1
- Day: 21 → 2+1 = 3  
- Year: 1985 → 1+9+8+5 = 23 → 2+3 = 5
- Life Path: 1+3+5 = 9
