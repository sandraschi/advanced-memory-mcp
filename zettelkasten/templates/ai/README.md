- Growing exponentially

### 🛡️ Ethics & Safety
**AI Ethics & Alignment** (`ethics-safety/ai-ethics-alignment.md`)

The challenge of aligning AI with human values:

**Alignment Problem**:
- Specification (outer alignment): Can't write down what we want
- Learning (inner alignment): Can't learn from behavior alone
- Robustness: Alignment may not generalize

**Current Approaches**:
- RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)
- Constitutional AI (Anthropic's approach)
- Interpretability (opening the black box)
- Inverse Reinforcement Learning

**Ethical Frameworks**:
- Utilitarianism (maximize happiness - wireheading problem)
- Deontology (follow rules - trolley problem)
- Preference learning (current dominant approach)

**Key Challenges**:
- Dual use (same AI for good and harm)
- Bias and fairness
- Privacy trade-offs
- Democratic governance

**Safety vs Capabilities Race**:
- Competitive pressure favors speed over safety
- Coordination problems
- No clear solutions

**Learn**:
- Why alignment is philosophically hard
- Current technical approaches
- Governance challenges
- What's at stake

## Key Themes Across Templates

- Change is exponential, not linear
- Timeline estimates vary wildly (AGI: 2030? 2050? 2100?)
- We're making civilizational decisions with incomplete information

### 2. **Power Concentration**
- ~10 people making decisions affecting 8 billion
- Corporate power exceeds most nations
- Democratic deficit in AI governance

### 3. **No Going Back**
- Can't un-invent AI
- Coordination problems (races to bottom)
- Window for intervention is closing

### 4. **Cassandras vs Pollyannas**
- Genuine expert disagreement
- Both could be right in different ways
- Your stance matters for what happens

### 5. **Distribution vs Invention**
- Technology isn't the only variable
- WHO benefits depends on policy, not just capabilities
- Same AI, very different futures possible
