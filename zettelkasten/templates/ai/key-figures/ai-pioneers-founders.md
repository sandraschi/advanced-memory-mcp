# AI Pioneers and Founders

The researchers, entrepreneurs, and visionaries who created and shaped artificial intelligence.

## Founding Fathers (Pre-1960)

### Alan Turing (1912-1954)
**The Father of Computer Science**

- **Key Contributions**:
  - Turing Machine (1936) - theoretical foundation of computation
  - Turing Test (1950) - test for machine intelligence
  - Breaking Enigma code (WWII)
- **Famous Quote**: "Can machines think?"
- **Paper**: "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" (1950)
- **Legacy**: Turing Award named after him
- **Tragedy**: Prosecuted for homosexuality, died age 41

### John McCarthy (1927-2011)
**The Father of AI**

- **Coined "Artificial Intelligence"** (1955)
- **Organized Dartmouth Conference** (1956) - birth of AI as field
- **Invented LISP** (1958) - first AI programming language
- **Contributions**: Time-sharing, garbage collection, circumscription logic
- **Philosophy**: Strong AI advocate - "even machines can be persons"
- **Legacy**: Stanford AI Lab, influenced generations

### Marvin Minsky (1927-2016)
**Co-founder of AI**

- **Co-founded MIT AI Lab** (1959)
- **Key Ideas**: Frames, Society of Mind theory
- **Book**: "The Society of Mind" (1986) - mind as collection of agents
- **Controversial**: "Perceptrons" (1969) - contributed to first AI winter
- **Quote**: "The brain is just a computer made of meat"
- **Impact**: Trained many AI leaders

### Claude Shannon (1916-2001)
**Father of Information Theory**

- **Information Theory** (1948) - foundation of digital communications
- **Chess-playing machines** (1950s)
- **Attended Dartmouth Conference**
- **Quote**: "I visualize a time when we will be to robots what dogs are to humans"
- **Style**: Playful genius - invented juggling machines, flame-throwing trumpet

## Deep Learning Revolution

### Geoffrey Hinton (1947-)
**Godfather of Deep Learning**

- **Backpropagation** (1986) - made neural networks trainable
- **Boltzmann Machines** (1983)
- **Capsule Networks** (2017)
- **Won Turing Award** (2018) with Bengio and LeCun
- **2023**: **Left Google to warn about AI risks**
- **Quote**: "I console myself with the normal excuse: If I hadn't done it, somebody else would have"
- **Current Position**: Now warns about existential risk

### Yann LeCun (1960-)
**Convolutional Neural Networks**

- **Invented CNNs** (1989) - revolutionized computer vision
- **Chief AI Scientist at Meta**
- **Won Turing Award** (2018)
- **Stance**: Very skeptical of AI extinction risk
- **Quote**: "Worrying about AI taking over is like worrying about overpopulation on Mars"
- **Advocacy**: Open source AI, dismisses

 doom scenarios

### Yoshua Bengio (1964-)
**Sequence Models Pioneer**

- **Sequence modeling, attention mechanisms**
- **Word embeddings** - foundation of modern NLP
- **Won Turing Award** (2018)
- **Shift**: Now focused on AI safety and beneficial AI
- **Quote**: "We're like alchemists who don't fully understand what we're creating"
- **Advocacy**: AI regulation, safety research

### Andrew Ng (1976-)
**AI Educator**

- **Co-founded Google Brain**
- **Founded Coursera** - democratized AI education
- **Chief Scientist at Baidu**
- **Stanford Prof** - famous Machine Learning course
- **Philosophy**: AI will benefit humanity, safety concerns overblown
- **Impact**: Trained millions in ML

### Ilya Sutskever (1986-)
**Scaling Hypothesis**

- **Co-creator of AlexNet** (2012) - started deep learning revolution
- **Co-founder and Chief Scientist of OpenAI**
- **Key insight**: "Scale is all you need" - bigger models work better
- **Led GPT development** (GPT-2, GPT-3)
- **2023**: Led board coup against Sam Altman, then reversed
- **Now**: Left OpenAI, started Safe Superintelligence Inc.
- **Quote**: "The best way to predict the future is to invent it"

## AI Company Founders

### Sam Altman (1985-)
**OpenAI CEO**

- **President of Y Combinator** (2014-2019)
- **CEO of OpenAI** (2019-present, except 5 days in Nov 2023)
- **Launched ChatGPT** - changed everything
- **Philosophy**: AGI is coming, race to build it safely
- **Controversial**: Fired by board, reinstated by employees/investors
- **Worldcoin**: Cryptocurrency project for AI era
- **Quote**: "Moore's law for everything" - vision of AI abundance

### Demis Hassabis (1976-)
**DeepMind Founder**

- **Child chess prodigy**, game designer, neuroscientist
- **Founded DeepMind** (2010), sold to Google (2014) for $500M
- **AlphaGo** - defeated world Go champion
- **AlphaFold** - solved protein folding
- **Nobel Prize** (2024, Chemistry) for AlphaFold
- **Philosophy**: AGI for scientific discovery
- **Approach**: Cautious, scientific, focused on beneficial outcomes

### Dario Amodei (1983-)
**Anthropic CEO**

- **VP Research at OpenAI** (left due to safety concerns)
- **Co-founded Anthropic** (2021) with sister Daniela
- **Claude**: Constitutional AI approach
- **Philosophy**: AI safety first, commercialization second
- **Approach**: Interpretability, honesty, harmlessness
- **Quote**: "The race to AGI should be a race to safe AGI"

### Mustafa Suleyman (1984-)
**DeepMind Co-founder**

- **Co-founded DeepMind** with Demis Hassabis
- **Left DeepMind** for Google in internal conflicts
- **Founded Inflection AI** (2022) - Pi chatbot
- **2024**: Joined Microsoft as AI CEO
- **Book**: "The Coming Wave" (2023) - AI and synthetic biology risks
- **Philosophy**: "Containment problem" - can't stop AI spread

## The Contrarians & Cassandras

### Eliezer Yudkowsky (1979-)
**AI Doomer-in-Chief**

- **Founded MIRI** (Machine Intelligence Research Institute)
- **Lesswrong** - rationalist community
- **Position**: >99% chance AI kills everyone without alignment solution
- **Advocacy**: International ban on AGI research above certain threshold
- **Quote**: "The AI does not love you, nor does it hate you, but you are made out of atoms"
- **Controversial**: Seen as too extreme even by safety researchers
- **Harry Potter fanfic**: "HPJEV" - teaching rationality

### Nick Bostrom (1973-)
**Superintelligence Scholar**

- **Director of Future of Humanity Institute** (Oxford)
- **Book**: "Superintelligence: Paths, Dangers, Strategies" (2014)
- **Simulation Hypothesis**: We might be in a simulation
- **Philosophy**: Existential risk from AI is serious
- **Impact**: Made AI risk intellectually respectable
- **Controversial**: Some past writings on sensitive topics

### Stuart Russell (1962-)
**Control Problem Researcher**

- **UC Berkeley Professor**
- **Wrote THE AI textbook** with Peter Norvig (used worldwide)
- **Book**: "Human Compatible" (2019) - AI alignment
- **Key idea**: Don't give AI fixed objectives, make it defer to humans
- **Quote**: "King Midas got exactly what he asked for"
- **Position**: Moderate - AI risk is real but solvable

## The Optimists (Pollyannas)

### Ray Kurzweil (1948-)
**Singularity Prophet**

- **Inventor**: Reading machine for blind, music synthesizers
- **Books**: "The Singularity is Near" (2005)
- **Prediction**: Singularity by 2045
- **Google**: Director of Engineering
- **Philosophy**: Transhumanism, merge with AI
- **Longevity**: Takes 150+ pills/day to live to singularity
- **Quote**: "Technology is evolution by other means"

### Marc Andreessen (1971-)
**"AI Will Save Us"**

- **Netscape founder**, legendary VC
- **"Techno-Optimist Manifesto"** (2023)
- **Position**: AI is pure good, regulation will kill it
- **Philosophy**: Technology solves all problems
- **Controversial**: Dismisses AI safety concerns
- **Quote**: "It's time to be optimistic"
- **Impact**: Funding AI startups aggressively

## AI Safety Researchers

### Paul Christiano (1987-)
**Alignment Optimist**

- **Founded Alignment Research Center**
- **Iterated amplification** approach
- **Worked at OpenAI** on safety
- **Philosophy**: Alignment is hard but tractable
- **Position**: ~10-20% AI extinction risk (lower than peers)
- **Approach**: Empirical, iterative

### Ajeya Cotra
**Timeline Researcher**

- **Open Philanthropy**
- **AI Timelines**: Influential forecasting work
- **Bio anchors framework**: Estimate compute needed for AGI
- **Estimates**: ~50% AGI by 2050
- **Impact**: Shaped EA community's AI strategy

## The Wildcards

### Elon Musk (1971-)
**Contradictory Genius**

- **Co-founded OpenAI** (2015) then left (2018)
- **Warned about AI danger** for years
- **Started xAI** (2023) - Grok
- **Neuralink**: Brain-computer interface
- **Contradiction**: Warns about AI while building it
- **Quote**: "AI is more dangerous than nuclear weapons"
- **Then**: Races to build AGI anyway

### Jensen Huang (1963-)
**NVIDIA CEO - Picks & Shovels**

- **Made GPUs that enabled AI revolution**
- **NVIDIA market cap**: >$3 trillion (2024)
- **Not AI researcher**, but enabled all of them
- **Quote**: "AI is the most powerful technology force of our time"
- **Impact**: Every AI lab depends on NVIDIA chips

## Generational Patterns

### First Generation (1950s-1960s)
- **Symbolic AI enthusiasts**
- **Over-optimistic** about timeline
- Turing, McCarthy, Minsky, Simon

### Second Generation (1980s-1990s)
- **Expert systems, then winter**
- Pearl, Hinton (early neural networks)

### Third Generation (2000s-2010s)
- **Deep learning revolution**
- Hinton, LeCun, Bengio
- Made AI actually work

### Fourth Generation (2020s)
- **Foundation models, commercialization**
- Altman, Amodei, Sutskever
- Building toward AGI

### Fifth Generation? (Future)
- **The AI safety generation?**
- Will determine if humanity survives AI

## Key Tensions

### Safety vs Capabilities
- Capabilities researchers: "Let's build it"
- Safety researchers: "Let's make it safe first"
- **Problem**: Capabilities advancing faster than safety

### Open vs Closed
- Open advocates: "Democratize AI"
- Closed advocates: "Too dangerous to share"
- **No consensus**

### Speed vs Caution
- Fast movers: "Race to AGI"
- Cautious: "Slow down"
- **Coordination problem**

### Profit vs Safety
- Commercial: "Ship products"
- Non-profit: "Long-term safety"
- **Misaligned incentives**
