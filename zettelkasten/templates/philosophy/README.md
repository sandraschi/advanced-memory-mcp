## Templates

### 🧠 Mind & Consciousness
**Philosophy of Mind** (`mind-consciousness/philosophy-of-mind.md`)

The study of consciousness, subjective experience, and the mind-body relationship:

**Mind-Body Problem**:
- Dualism (Descartes): Mind and body separate substances
- Physicalism: Everything is physical
- Property Dualism: Mental properties emerge from physical
- Panpsychism: Consciousness is fundamental

**The Hard Problem** (David Chalmers):
- Easy problems: Functional mechanisms (solvable)
- Hard problem: Why subjective experience? (mystery)
- Explanatory gap: Physical → Conscious experience
- Philosophical zombies thought experiment

**Theories of Consciousness**:
- Global Workspace Theory (Baars)
- Integrated Information Theory (Tononi) - Φ measure
- Higher-Order Thought Theory
- Attention Schema Theory (Graziano)

**Qualia**:
- Mary's Room (Frank Jackson)
- Inverted spectrum problem
- What it's like to be a bat (Nagel)

**AI Consciousness**:
- Can machines be conscious?
- Chinese Room argument (Searle)
- Functionalism implications
- Current status of LLMs

**Other Topics**:

### 📚 Theory of Knowledge
**Epistemology** (`epistemology/theory-of-knowledge.md`)

The study of knowledge, belief, and justification:

**Classical Definition**:
- Knowledge as Justified True Belief (JTB)
- Three conditions: Belief, Truth, Justification

**Gettier Problem** (1963):
- JTB is not sufficient for knowledge
- Famous counterexamples
- Destroyed 2000-year consensus

**Theories of Justification**:
- Foundationalism: Knowledge has foundations
- Coherentism: Knowledge as coherent web
- Reliabilism: Knowledge from reliable processes

**Skepticism**:
- External world skepticism
- Descartes' method of doubt
- Brain in vat / simulation hypothesis
- Responses: Moore, contextualism

**A Priori vs A Posteriori**:
- Rationalism: Knowledge from reason
- Empiricism: Knowledge from experience
- Kant's synthesis: Synthetic a priori

**Problem of Induction**:
- Hume's challenge
- Cannot justify induction without circularity
- Responses: Pragmatic, probabilistic, falsificationism

**Social Epistemology**:
- Testimony and trust
- Epistemic injustice
- Misinformation and epistemic crisis
